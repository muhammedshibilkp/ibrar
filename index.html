Kashmiri Culture & Heritage

Kashmir is famous for its traditional handicrafts, Pashmina shawls, beautiful carpets, Kashmiri music, traditional clothing and delicious Wazwan cuisine. Its culture reflects hospitality, creativity and heritage.
